### 🧠 Executive Summary & Research Profile

> *"I engineer resilient, energy-adaptive AI systems at the intersection of Reinforcement Learning, Autonomous Multi-Agent architectures, and Embedded Edge Intelligence — from rigorous mathematical formulation through production deployment."*

I am a **B.Tech scholar in Industrial IoT (Data Science & ML) at VIPS-TC, Delhi (CGPA: 8.67/10)**, an **AI/ML Researcher with a published Springer paper**, a **published patent applicant**, and an **Open Source Developer at LLM4S Foundation**. My core focus is developing resource-efficient machine learning algorithms for real-world hardware constraints, battery-free IoT networks, bio-inspired computing benchmark environments, and autonomous agent pipelines.

- 🔬 **Edge Intelligence & Resource-Efficient RL** — Author of **TinyRLAM**, published in Springer Nature *Wireless Personal Communications* (2026). Specializing in sub-milliwatt on-device RL policies for energy-harvesting wireless sensor networks (EH-WSNs).
- 📜 **Intellectual Property & Hardware Innovation** — Published patent applicant for an *Optically Controlled Acoustic Phase Matrix System* and registered design holder for a *Portable AI Diagnostic Device* (Indian Patent Office).
- 🤝 **Production Open Source Engineering** — Active contributor to the **LLM4S Foundation** with 10+ PRs merged into production across TypeScript and Scala LLM tooling.
- 🧬 **Bio-Inspired Computing & Gym Environments** — Simulating organoid neural dynamics and Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) for reinforcement learning benchmarks (**OrganoidEnv**), achieving 95%+ CNN parity at >10x lower energy.
- 🤖 **Autonomous Multi-Agent Architectures** — Developing deterministic, audit-ready multi-agent systems (**Healthcare Agent**, **Abstractify**) combining semantic citation graphs, tool augmentations, and structured verification.
