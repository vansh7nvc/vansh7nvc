### 🧠 Executive Summary & Research Frontier

> *"I engineer intelligent, adaptive systems at the intersection of Reinforcement Learning, Autonomous Multi-Agent architectures, and scalable Full-Stack engineering."*

I am a **Software Engineer and AI/ML Researcher** focused on developing resource-efficient machine learning algorithms for real-world constraints and high-throughput web applications. My work spans from **Edge Reinforcement Learning** for energy-harvesting IoT networks to **Autonomous Agent Systems** and **Semantic Synthesis Engines**.

- 🔬 **Reinforcement Learning & Edge Intelligence** — Developing resource-constrained RL policies (TinyRLAM, Q-Learning, PPO) capable of running on low-power microcontrollers and EH-WSNs without cloud dependency.
- 🧬 **Bio-Inspired Computing & Gym Environments** — Simulating organoid intelligence and neural-computational dynamics for reinforcement learning benchmark environments (Organoid-Intelligence-gym).
- 🤖 **Agentic AI & Semantic Intelligence** — Building autonomous multi-agent pipelines (Healthcare Agent, Abstractify) combining LLM reasoning chains, graph-based citations, and structured extraction.
- 💻 **Full-Stack Architecture & Systems** — Engineering resilient, modern web platforms utilizing **TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, Express, and MongoDB/PostgreSQL**.
- 🌿 **Green & Sustainable Computing** — Benchmarking energy footprints and compute efficiency in neural network architectures (Green-AI).
